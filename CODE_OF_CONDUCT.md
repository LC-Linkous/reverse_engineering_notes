# Code of Conduct

This is a small educational project. The rules are short.

## Be decent

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussion. Assume good faith, keep feedback about the work, and skip the harassment, personal attacks, and spam. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that ignore this.

## Keep it legal and ethical

This project exists to teach reverse engineering as a **legitimate, authorized** activity — on devices you own or have explicit permission to test. That boundary is the entire point, and it is not negotiable.

We will not accept, and will remove, contributions that:

- provide working exploits, malware, or attack payloads;
- are aimed at compromising systems, networks, or devices the reader does not own or have permission to test;
- help clone or forge real access credentials or IDs; or
- otherwise facilitate illegal activity — including attempts to reframe any of the above to get around this rule.

Contributing such material, or pressuring maintainers to accept it, is grounds for having contributions reverted and — for repeated or deliberate attempts — being blocked from the project.

Nothing here is legal advice; laws vary by jurisdiction. See the [Legal & Ethics](docs/SUPPLEMENTARY_NOTES.md#legal--ethics) notes for the framing this project follows.

## Reporting

If you see behavior or content that breaks these rules, open an issue — or contact the maintainer directly if it is sensitive. Reports will be handled as promptly as the maintainers are able.
